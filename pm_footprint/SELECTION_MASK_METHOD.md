**Status (2026-10-31): the JWST star-mask sky union and its overlap with exact public epoch polygons have provisional Monte Carlo measurements; NO F444W=26.2 AB dropout-selection completeness area or exact WHT∩star-mask area has been measured.**

## What is already measured

- Official COSMOS-Web DR1 F444W 60mas WHT>0 approximate geometric coverage (20 tiles); **do not rerun the 7 GB workflow** merely to check status.
- A 365-day F444W+red geometric opportunity of **0.314624457 deg²** within **0.528823685 deg²** WHT>0, or **59.495%**, and 62/125 source entries. The WHT rasterizer has a documented systematic native-to-grid area loss of about **0.96%**, so these are not sub-percent-accurate physical areas.

## Why there is not one universally correct 26.2 AB usable-area mask

For a *specified* source F444W magnitude m, brown-dwarf SED/model s, sky position x and measurement procedure p, define

    C_drop(x; m,s,p) =
      P(F444W detection passes threshold AND F115W,F150W,F277W
        non-detections pass threshold AND adopted F444W/F277W
        color/flux-ratio criterion passes AND quality flags pass
        | x,m,s,p).

This probability varies with source flux, SED and local depth. A binary mask from WHT>0, or a per-tile WHT relative-median cut, is **not** a substitute. If the 26.2 AB selection is described as an F444W *limiting magnitude*, it must be paired with a specified detection S/N definition and noise/aperture correction; do not equate an individual catalog object's native-resolution aperture SNR with the local limiting depth. Dave's original 125-candidate selection may use *auto-flux/error* rather than the catalog's native-resolution aperture SNR column. Verify the exact selection code before calibrating the noise model.

For an assumed source-population distribution p(m,s), a well-defined **effective dropout selection area** is

    A_eff_drop = integral_over_sky [ integral C_drop(x;m,s,p) p(m,s) dm ds ] dOmega.

For a fixed source model and magnitude, use A_eff_drop(m,s) without averaging. Injection/recovery with the same source extraction and selection is the preferred calibration. As a less complete approximation, construct a calibrated 5-sigma limiting-magnitude map from noise/ERR and empirical random-aperture noise, apply an explicit source model and dropout thresholds, and report the approximation as such.

For proper-motion **measurement opportunity** with epoch requirement T and filter policy k, define G_T,k(x) as the previously measured binary multi-epoch geometric mask. The relevant denominator and numerator are

    f_covered(T,k;m,s) =
      integral G_T,k(x) C_drop(x;m,s,p) dOmega
      / integral C_drop(x;m,s,p) dOmega.

This is a *selection-weighted geometrical overlap*, **not** the probability of measurable proper motion. To estimate PM detectability, additionally model/measure both-epoch detection probability, positional uncertainty, astrometric registration, time baseline and a PM significance threshold. Cross-filter detections of F444W-only red dropouts can be especially incomplete.

## Quantitative F277W color-limit sanity check (2026-10-30)

**Conditional on the original Dave selection using `F444W/F277W > 30`:** this flux-density ratio implies `m_F277W - m_F444W > 2.5 log10(30) = 3.6928 mag`. At the nominal faint boundary `m_F444W = 26.2 AB`, a **2-sigma upper limit** on F277W must be fainter than `m_F277W,2sigma = 29.8928 AB` to *establish* the >30 ratio from non-detection alone. For Gaussian noise and a consistent aperture, this requires a 5-sigma F277W limiting magnitude of `m_F277W,5sigma >= 28.898 AB`.

The [official COSMOS-Web planned point-source F277W 5-sigma depths](https://cosmos.astro.caltech.edu/page/cosmosweb) span **27.71–28.28 AB** for one to four exposures. Converted with `2.5 log10(5/2) = 0.99485 mag`, the corresponding **nominal** 2-sigma limits are **28.705–29.275 AB**. At F444W=26.2, these limits establish only `F444W/F277W > 10.0–17.0`, not >30. Conversely, to *prove* a >30 ratio using those nominal 2-sigma limits, F444W must be brighter than roughly **25.01–25.58 AB**, depending on local F277W depth. These are *illustrative nominal-depth arithmetic*, **not** a measured area, and the planned depths may differ from local DR1 empirical noise.

This is a crucial distinction between (a) selecting objects whose *measured*, possibly low-SNR F277W flux gives a ratio >30 and (b) establishing that ratio at a specified confidence level using a 2-sigma F277W upper limit. The two selection functions are not equivalent. Negative/noisy fluxes can also make a measured flux ratio unstable. Before implementing any binary 26.2-AB dropout mask, **inspect the actual Dave selection code** for its treatment of negative F277W flux, flux errors, 2-sigma upper limits, and whether the >30 ratio is a hard cut or descriptive statistic. If the cut is based on measured flux, use an explicit noise/selection simulation rather than asserting that all nondetections meet a secure >30 color limit. Do not infer that any of the 125 entries fail the actual selection from this calculation alone.

## Public-data acquisition plan (avoid local workstation first)

1. **Candidate quality and catalog version QC.** Query the public CDS/VizieR J/A+A/704/A339/phot table for the **125 known IDs only** (separate cosmosweb_catalog_qc.yml workflow). Check 0.5-arcsec coordinate agreement with frozen Dave125 positions, compare F444W aperture SNR/weights and blue-band values, and record flag-star, flag-blend, and quality columns when present. The CDS publication copy may not match the updated COSMOS2025 v1.1 catalog or Dave's selection catalog. Do not merge IDs without astrometric validation.
2. **Obtain the official spatial JWST starmask.** The release homepage states the JWST and HSC starmasks are downloadable under catalog/download, but the current download gateway presents a registration/captcha form. Do not invent a static file URL. If obtainable, inspect its coordinate system and semantics and rasterize *mask polygons*, not catalog flag-star values. Also inspect other exclusion masks and the original Dave selection cuts.
**Why the published spatial mask itself is essential (Shuntov et al. 2026, A&A, DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202555799, Section 3.2):** The COSMOS2025 team did **not** use a simple circular exclusion around catalogued bright stars. They generated extended NIRCam PSF-derived region templates, automatically selected masks for stars with F150W <= 17 AB based on segmentation, rotated/transferred the templates to observation orientation, **manually adjusted** individual regions, and **manually added** scattered-light artifacts and some diffuse nearby galaxies. The paper explicitly describes large diffraction spikes and a B10 *Dragon's Breath* artifact. Therefore a Gaia bright-star radius cut or a `flag_star` source-count fraction is **not a validated substitute** for the actual spatial JWST mask; it would miss nonstellar artifacts and cannot reproduce hand-edited boundaries. Obtain the official mask file(s) with original region/WCS provenance and intersect them with the archived epoch polygons. Preserve a separate candidate-level flag audit; do not infer spatial area from catalog flags. [Published masking procedure](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202555799), [official release announcing mask](https://cosmos2025.iap.fr/), [download gateway](https://cosmos2025.iap.fr/catalog_download.php). No direct mask URL has been verified; do not guess or bypass the access form.

3. **F444W 26.2 depth.** Determine the actual PSF/aperture-based 5-sigma threshold from the official ERR/WHT + empirical noise methodology, *not* from arbitrary relative WHT fractions. Verify whether 26.2 is the catalog selection magnitude limit or merely a model truncation. The released DR1 WHT has spatial exposure weights, not an absolute point-source completeness curve.
4. **Blue non-detections.** Include F115W/F150W/F277W depth maps or source injection/noise simulations for brown-dwarf SEDs; a deeper blue image can *reduce* the chance of meeting a non-detection criterion for a fixed SED, so the joint selection footprint cannot be derived from F444W depth alone.
5. **Critical COSMOS2025 quality-flag selection trap.** The official [catalog documentation](https://cosmos2025.iap.fr/catalog.html) defines `warn_flag=4` as a *single-NIRCam-band detection*, classified as potentially instrumental (17,520 entries in the released catalog). Dave's **F444W-only** dropout selection can intentionally produce that same photometric pattern. Therefore, **do not automatically require `warn_flag=0` or remove `warn_flag=4` without inspecting the exact original selection and independent image/PM evidence**: doing so could systematically exclude the very population under study. Conversely, do not assume every single-band candidate is genuine. The published COSMOS2025 source detection is performed on a multi-band detection image, with both hot and cold modes; its `warn_flag` is not a spatial area mask. Keep `flag_star`, `flag_blend`, `warn_flag` and independent imaging checks as separate, source-level QC outputs. The same documentation says `flag_star` encodes a 0–80% overlap of source pixels with the JWST star mask and `flag_blend` identifies large F444W Kron aperture corrections (>3). Neither can be converted into a masked sky area by counting flagged sources.
6. **Crowding and quality.** The 125 entries form 115 positionally linked groups within 0.5 arcsec (62 red-covered entries form 60 groups). This is not an automatic duplicate-removal rule. Inspect flag-blend, flag-star, segmentation, and visual cutouts before treating each as an independent source.
7. **Area/raster precision.** Correct or independently bound the ~0.96% native-to-grid area deposition/clipping loss before claiming a precise WHT>0 denominator. Post-rasterization grid coarsening alone is not independent native 60mas convergence. Reprocess original 7 GB **only** if the revised algorithm has passed synthetic native-pixel area conservation tests and a clearly justified need for higher precision.
8. **Provenance.** Keep the official DR1 WHT mask, any alternate PSF-matched v13 WHT, and any 0.49672 deg² usable-area estimate separate unless their selection, WCS, processing and sky masks are demonstrated equivalent.

## 2026-10-31: measured JWST masked sky-union (still not selection-matched)

The **user-uploaded** twenty-tile JWST starmask has now been processed not only for 125 source centers (0/125 masked; ID 3153521 closest at 0.666 arcsec), but also for a first *overlap-aware unique-sky masked-area estimate*. The native **summed per-tile masked area 0.0141992 deg² is NOT a union**. Independent 0.3- and 0.6-arcsec fractional Lambert equal-area grids, with bilinear area-conserving deposition and per-cell max versus clipped sum across overlapping tiles, yield a **provisional JWST masked sky union of ~0.0128–0.0131 deg²**, or roughly **0.013 deg²**. Per-tile deposition conserves an average 99.375% at 0.3 arcsec and 99.913% at 0.6 arcsec; upper/lower values are raster *approximations*, not strict native-pixel geometric inequalities. See [computed numerical summary](results/jwst_starmask/union_area_grid_qc.json), [reproduction code](jwst_mask_union_repro.py), and [issue #1](https://github.com/physicsbahare/BD_number_density/issues/1#issuecomment-6541203105).

**Do NOT subtract 0.013 deg² blindly** from the archival/WHT multi-epoch areas: the quantity is an approximate sky union over the *mask-tile region*, not the **intersection** of mask with the calibrated F444W WHT-positive footprint or a multi-epoch red-band polygon. The latter must be spatially intersected with compatible WCS and the known WHT raster deposition loss still addressed. An independent native-pixel or offset/resolution test and confirmation that code 1 means excluded remain publication QC. The F444W=26.2 AB dropout selection function, including blue-band depths and blend flags, remains unsolved; **no selection-matched area is claimed**.

## 2026-10-31: first measured JWST star-mask ∩ >=365d archival epoch geometry

The uploaded **20-tile COSMOS2025 JWST binary starmask** was processed directly (no re-upload or MAST/WHT download). We have now gone beyond merely counting 125 source centers: a reproducible **0.6-arcsec equal-area, clipped-sum union-mask raster** was importance-sampled at **1,000 masked-sky positions** (fixed seed 20261031) and each sample point independently tested against **all 717 PUBLIC red-band NIRCam imaging CAOM polygons** using exact observation-interval separations. The independent polygon evaluator was validated by a **200×200 uniform equal-area survey grid**: its F444W survey area differs from the saved exact-Shapely result by 0.0082%, and the >=365d red area by 0.119%. This validation does not remove the mask-raster or WHT systematic errors.

**New preliminary measurements:** The 0.6-arcsec JWST mask union raster spans **0.01285985–0.01307118 deg²** under per-cell max versus clipped-sum approximations. Using the latter as the MC sampling distribution, **995/1000** mask samples lie inside the COSMOS-Web F444W *archival* polygon, **575/1000** lie inside the **>=365d F444W+red archival overlap**, and **70/1000** inside repeated F444W archival overlap. These imply an estimated star-mask intersection with the archival survey of **0.013006 deg²**, and with the >=365d red archival overlap of **0.007516 deg²** (MC-only 95% half-width ~**0.000400 deg²**; raster/systematic uncertainty extra). The **archive-only** F444W area changes from 0.533899 to approximately **0.520893 deg²**, and the red-overlap area from 0.317487 to approximately **0.309971 deg²**. The resulting **archive-only mask-corrected geometric fraction is ~59.51%**, not a selection-matched area.

**Critical WHT distinction:** The previously computed 0.528824 deg² WHT>0 and 0.314624 deg² WHT+red do **not** have their spatial WHT grid saved. Therefore **do not subtract the archival mask intersections from the WHT areas as though exact**. We can, however, use known archive–WHT differences *separately inside and outside* the red overlap to obtain conditional set-theoretic bounds. Given the above sampled mask areas and the nesting built into `wht_depth_mask.evaluate()`, the star-mask-corrected **WHT>0 >=365d red geometric fraction is bounded approximately between 59.28% and 59.76%**. These are **conditional bounds from approximate MC inputs**, not a measured spatial WHT∩mask intersection; MC sampling, raster, and the documented ~0.96% WHT deposition bias preclude sub-percent physical precision.

The published source-center result **0/125** in masked pixels is unchanged, and 62/125 are in the geometric >=365d red overlap. Neither establishes actual F356W detection or PM measurability. The **F444W=26.2 dropout selection function**, F277W upper-limit/color cut, blue-band depth maps, and source flags are still uncalibrated.
