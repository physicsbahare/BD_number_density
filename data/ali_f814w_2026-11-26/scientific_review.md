## Follow-up: source photon noise and empirical-CDF uncertainty

- **Source-noise algebra is correct under the stated centered-aperture/local-gain approximation.** `ali_count_run.py:62-64` forms q = (sum(PSF*k^2)/sum(PSF*k))*shot_coefficient/WHT. q has nJy units; source aperture flux times q is aperture variance. `ali_spatial_counts.py:53-58,70-75` adds this variance to Gaussian noise, or convolves the empirical blank distribution with independent source noise. The Hermite expression in `ali_count_run.py:130-135` has the correct normalization, scale and sign. It adds source noise once.
- **Donor provenance and distance guard are fixed.** `ali_count_run.py:14-25` copies ACS properties and photon-noise coefficient only inside covered cells, enforces a maximum distance, and records donor tile identity. Grouping and CDF selection retain that identity.
- **Uncertainty accumulation is mathematically sound for a step ECDF.** `ali_selection_uncertainty.py:44-57` accumulates each training blank's total contribution over temperature bins, radial nodes, spatial cells and source-noise nodes before taking sample variance/n. This preserves dependence induced by reusing a blank across bins. Reverse cumulative masses correctly implement the indicator that a residual is below threshold.
- **Minimum consistency fix:** main counts and validation use linearly interpolated ECDFs (`ali_count_run.py:134-135`, `ali_empirical_validation.py:29`), but the uncertainty estimator uses a step ECDF (`ali_selection_uncertainty.py:53-55`). The reported SE therefore applies to a slightly different estimator. Use the same ECDF convention, or report the count difference and show it is negligible relative to the SE. Linear interpolation exceeds the right-continuous step ECDF between samples by at most 1/n within a stratum.
- **Minimum convergence fix:** uncertainty hardcodes 15 Hermite nodes (`ali_selection_uncertainty.py:37`); main counts permit a configurable node count and validation uses 21. Record and synchronize the chosen nodes, and verify 15 versus a larger count. Verify uncertainty radial Legendre convergence separately (e.g. 256 versus 512); piecewise ECDF integrands are not guaranteed accurate from one fixed-node run. The uncertainty script's central count should agree with the corresponding main count to well below its reported SE.
- **Conditional SE limitations:** variance/n assumes independent blank apertures and independent strata. It conditions on noise curves, fitted catalog estimator, PSF, gain, radial convention, grouping and estimated WHT strata. It is not a complete selection uncertainty and should not absorb those systematics. Shared/nearby blank apertures across overlapping tiles may weaken the independence assumption.
- **Validation qualification:** disjoint even/odd held-out scenes remove direct reuse of ECDF scenes in validation. The reported level-2 difference remains a diagnostic discrepancy, not a significance claim based only on binomial SE; include ECDF-estimation uncertainty before interpreting it. `ali_empirical_validation.py:23-25` reconstructs source variance by inverting saved Gaussian probabilities; this is numerically ill-conditioned near probability 0, 1 or 0.5. Saving the actual injected source variance would be preferable; no large error is established in the current diagnostic.
- **Negligible coordinate issue in checked tiles:** second PSF moment uses `d['grid_xy']` (`ali_count_run.py:62`) whereas the original ACS EE used native grid coordinates (`ali_depth_analysis.py:124,184`). On A1/B1 native-minus-reference grid offset is median (-1,0) pixel; maximum fractional second-moment difference is only 8.95e-8/8.08e-7. This is not a material blocker in these checks. Centered/unweighted q and EE still differ from the phase-aware weighted injection estimator, as acknowledged.

## Outstanding scientific limitations from original review

1. **Radial estimand:** default `ali_spatial_counts.py:43-44` truncates at intrinsic flux = 5*empirical blank sigma, then applies catalog-SNR probabilities inside that domain. Final-cache median sigma_noise/sigma_snr is 2.316 on A1 and 2.360 on B1 in F444W: the boundary corresponds to native SNR about 11.6-11.8. A native-5-sigma intrinsic reach would be about 1.52 times farther. `catalog_depth` is a useful sensitivity convention, not recovery of the missing historical implementation.
2. **ACS estimator residual:** final held-out F814W median absolute SNR errors span 0.412-0.563 across 20 tiles; A1 residual p16/p50/p84 = -0.647/0.115/0.710 and B1 = -0.924/-0.040/0.761. An exact-native-estimator claim needs qualification and a count sensitivity to these residuals.
3. **Band covariance:** product marginal probabilities assume independence. Across 83,286 jointly matched held-out NIR blanks, retaining all four abs(z)<5, F277W-F444W residual correlation is 0.1671. Other pairs range 0.0273-0.0526. Bound its effect on detection plus blue veto before publication.
4. **Source aperture response:** count EE is centered and unweighted; actual estimator is subpixel and sqrt(WHT)-weighted. At 100 spaced ACS held-out positions, weighted/centered EE p16/p50/p84 = 0.953/0.983/1.065 (A1), 0.946/0.977/1.031 (B1), with extrema 0.858-1.216. Report a sensitivity rather than assuming exact equivalence.
5. **Positive AUTO condition:** among 660,730 catalog entries with native F444W SNR>5, 47 have nonpositive/nonfinite F444W AUTO; among 1,227 passing all five signed SNR cuts, one does. The observed effect is small, but the count does not explicitly model this separate catalog criterion. Quality flags are a different completeness term: 1,182 of those 1,227 have warn_flag nonzero; include only if required by the frozen selection scope.
6. **Coverage precision:** fixed centered aperture support in native footprint erosion approximates fractional-center apertures. Cached SCI/ERR validity masks are optional. Quantify footprint sensitivity and confirm all required validity caches exist.
7. **Uncertainty scope:** density errors plus quadrature and finite-CDF sampling errors do not include all selection and population systematics. Lower-limit temperature bins remain separately flagged; do not present their absent density error as zero physical uncertainty.
